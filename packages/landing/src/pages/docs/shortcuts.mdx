If Cadencr is where you keep work moving, shortcuts help you spend less time reaching for the mouse.

You do **not** need to memorize every key combo on day one. Learn the few that save you the most time, then add more as you go.

<DocCallout title="Quick help" variant="tip">
  If you forget a shortcut, press <Kbd>⌘</Kbd>
  <Kbd>/</Kbd> to open the in-app shortcuts help.
</DocCallout>

## Why shortcuts matter here

Cadencr has a few surfaces you bounce between a lot: the sidebar, feature workspace, agent session, editor, terminal, and unified agent list.

The faster you can move between those places, the easier it is to stay focused on the work itself.

A few good shortcuts help you:

- switch views without losing your train of thought,
- stop or steer an agent quickly when it gets adventurous,
- open files and search faster than point-and-click wandering, and
- keep your hands on the keyboard when you are already in the zone.

## A few habit-building tips

### 1. Learn three anchors first

If you only learn three things this week, make them:

- <Kbd>⌘</Kbd>
  <Kbd>K</Kbd> for command palette,
- <Kbd>⌘</Kbd>
  <Kbd>⇧</Kbd>
  <Kbd>A</Kbd>, <Kbd>⌘</Kbd>
  <Kbd>⇧</Kbd>
  <Kbd>T</Kbd>, <Kbd>⌘</Kbd>
  <Kbd>⇧</Kbd>
  <Kbd>G</Kbd>, and <Kbd>⌘</Kbd>
  <Kbd>⇧</Kbd>
  <Kbd>E</Kbd> for tab switching, and
- <Kbd>⌘</Kbd>
  <Kbd>/</Kbd> for help.

That little trio covers a surprising amount of daily life.

### 2. Use the keyboard to move, not just to type

A lot of people use shortcuts only for search. In Cadencr, the bigger win is navigation.

If you practice moving between areas with <Kbd>⌘</Kbd><Kbd>⌥</Kbd><Kbd>←</Kbd> and <Kbd>⌘</Kbd><Kbd>⌥</Kbd><Kbd>→</Kbd>, the app starts feeling much easier to navigate.

### 3. Keep one "save me time" shortcut in another tab of your brain

When in doubt:

- use <Kbd>⌘</Kbd><Kbd>P</Kbd> to find a file,
- use <Kbd>⌘</Kbd><Kbd>⇧</Kbd><Kbd>F</Kbd> to find text,
- use <Kbd>⌘</Kbd><Kbd>G</Kbd> to inspect what the agent changed.

Those three shortcuts solve a lot of common “where is it?” moments.
