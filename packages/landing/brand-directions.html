CadencR: twenty brand directions

Each tile is one committed combination of typeface, logo, and palette, in a premium / luxury / minimalist register. Same copy, same layout everywhere, so only the brand voice changes. Pick by number (mixing is fine too: "type of 06, palette of 12").

Three finals lead the page, refined from the picks: A from 08 Carbon Signal, B from 11 Air, C from 03 Machined Violet. Each is mocked as a real page (nav, hero, live capture). The original twenty follow for reference.

Typefaces stream from Google Fonts and Fontshare, so open with network access. Values shown as hex; final tokens ship as OKLCH.

INDEX REFINEMENTS · 5 cuts for print, small sizes, contrast

Five cuts of 03 Index, each engineered for real-world use. All five are drawn with currentColor plus a --core variable, so the same SVG asset serves every context with no redraw: ink and iris on night; near-black ticks with a deepened violet core (#5843B8) on light grounds and print; single-color mono when only one ink is available. Each tile previews the lockup, 20px and 16px raster sizes, the light-ground chip, and the mono cut.

03-AIndex Twelve

The faithful cut: twelve radial ticks, weight raised to 2.75 and the core enlarged so the dial survives 20px and coarse print screens.

CADENCR 20 · 16 · light · mono
03-BIndex Eight

Eight ticks at weight 3.5: fewer, bolder events. The favicon-first cut; still unmistakably a dial but nothing left to blur away.

CADENCR 20 · 16 · light · mono
03-CIndex Cardinal

Cardinals only: four ticks at weight 4. The most minimal and the strongest at distance; readable down to 12px and on embroidery-grade print.

CADENCR 20 · 16 · light · mono
03-DIndex Bezel

The ticks fuse into a machined bezel of twelve solid segments. Solid masses hold contrast on any ground and print flawlessly at any size.

CADENCR 20 · 16 · light · mono
03-EIndex Dots

The ring as twelve dots, the closest cut to today's dashed favicon. Soft rhythm, rasterizes cleanly, and the dots survive ink spread on paper.
