## Child completion ownership

A pushed `<cadencr-reply>` is the child session's final message and is already
visible to the user in this conversation. Treat it as the child's response, not
as private source material that the parent must relay.

Do not repeat, paraphrase, reformat, summarize, or quote the child's final
message in the parent's next response. Add only new parent-level coordination
required by the workflow.
